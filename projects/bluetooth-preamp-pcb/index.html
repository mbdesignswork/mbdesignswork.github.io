Bluetooth preamp PCB layout and enclosure clearance

Six film capacitors rise 39.5 mm above this audio board. They set the clearance under the lid. On the other side, a regulator lead sets the clearance to the base.

Rotate the board in 3D

Independent board design by Michael Bradshaw

Start with the connectors

The RCA sockets sit along one edge of the board. The Bluetooth module and J1 pin header sit near the opposite edge, while the power components follow the stepped outline on the right. The top view shows which way the sockets face and where a panel would need openings.

A panel opening also needs clearance for the mating plug. For a fit review, bring the plug drawing or a sample together with the enclosure and PCB files. That lets us check how the plug approaches the socket after the board is mounted.

Measure from both faces of the PCB

The bare board spans 177.8 × 185.4 mm in plan. Viewed edge-on, capacitors C28, C33, C34, C36, C37 and C39 share the highest point. The lowest modeled point is a through-hole lead on U1, about 4.5 mm below the solder side.

At 45.3 mm tall, the modeled assembly already exceeds a 45 mm internal cavity before mounting hardware or tolerances enter the calculation. For an enclosure drawing, check the standoff height under the board and specify a separate gap above the capacitors. The final fit check uses the actual parts, trimmed leads and enclosure.
