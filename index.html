How it works

A clear process for each prototype stage.

You can start with a rough idea, an old board, a breadboard circuit, or a half-working prototype. The first goal is to understand the problem, pick the right technical path, and avoid wasted build time.

01

Send the details

Describe the intended use, what you need, your available files, budget, and target timing. Mention any confidentiality requirements before sharing sensitive files.

02

Agree the scope and terms

Confirm deliverables, testing limits, exclusions, price, and schedule. After proposal acceptance, agree a short services agreement before payment or work begins.

03

Approve and fund the work

Receive the payment link for the agreed service, hourly credit or monthly subscription. Work starts on the agreed schedule; additional hours, scope changes, and outside costs require approval.

04

Review the deliverables

Receive the agreed files, models, or technical notes, with completed checks, known limitations, and any further testing or revisions identified for the next stage. Monthly clients also receive an hours and progress summary to plan the next priorities.

Rates & billing

Choose monthly support or project-based work.

Monthly support: CA$750/month for 10 hours. Reserved capacity, an agreed task list, tracked time and a monthly progress summary. See the monthly plan. Billing dates, cancellation, unused-hour treatment and scheduling are confirmed in your subscription agreement before enrollment.

Hourly project work

Project support for new clients starts at CA$70/hr. After the scope and services agreement are accepted, hourly work begins with a 2-hour prepaid minimum of CA$140. Time is deducted as work is completed; the initial credit is not a fixed price for completing an entire project.

Work pauses when the available credit is used. Further work requires approval and prepayment of a minimum 2-hour top-up at the agreed rate. Scope changes and additional costs are confirmed before they are incurred.

For hourly work, the credit policy is confirmed in the project agreement before payment: when a project finishes, unused credit of 2 hours or less stays on file for future work; more than 2 unused hours can be refunded on request or retained as credit. This policy is subject to any refund or cancellation rights required by applicable law.

Pricing covers design and support labor only. Components, PCB manufacturing, 3D print material, print time, outside fabrication, shipping, specialty modules, and replacement parts are separate unless clearly included in a written quote.

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Existing clients retain their agreed project rates; use the payment link supplied for your approved scope or invoice. Contact Michael if you need the appropriate link resent.

FAQ

Common questions before starting.

Monthly support, project scope, pricing and working together remotely.

What does the monthly subscription include?

CA$750 CAD reserves 10 hours per month across electronics, PCB, firmware, CAD and prototype support. We agree the task priorities and schedule, track the time and review progress monthly. Project calls, testing and documentation count toward those hours. Materials and outside costs are separate.

Is a complete PCB or product included for CA$750?

The monthly plan reserves time. A complete design may require multiple months or a separate project scope, depending on complexity and testing. We estimate the work and agree milestones before starting. Additional hours or outside costs require approval.

How do renewal, cancellation and unused hours work?

The plan bills monthly. Renewal timing, cancellation and the treatment of unused hours are stated in the subscription agreement before enrollment. The hourly project-credit policy is separate. We confirm capacity and scope before sending your subscription payment link.

Can we work together from the US or another country?

Yes. MB Designs Work is based in Canada and welcomes US, Canadian and worldwide clients. Design files, reviews and progress updates can be handled remotely; any physical prototypes, shipping and time-zone requirements are agreed for the project. Published prices are in CAD.

Who is behind MB Designs Work?

Michael Bradshaw runs MB Designs Work. He has studied Electrical Engineering & Management at McMaster University and has practical experience in electronics manufacturing support, PCB work, testing, debugging, CAD fixtures, 3D printing, and prototyping.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

How do prepaid credits and top-ups work?

Scope and the services agreement come first. Hourly work for new clients then starts with a CA$140 (2-hour) prepaid credit at CA$70/hr. Work pauses when credit is used and resumes after an approved top-up at the agreed rate. See Rates & billing for unused-credit and separate-cost terms.

Can you design the full PCB for me?

Yes. Services include schematic capture, component selection, Altium PCB layout and manufacturing-file preparation. We first define the intended use, interfaces, complexity and testing requirements, then agree the deliverables, project phases and schedule. See the project scope for current service limits.

Do you do firmware and Bluetooth?

Yes. Work includes embedded C, microcontroller peripherals, sensor interfaces, serial communication, motor-control logic, Bluetooth module integration and hardware testing. Can you reverse engineer an old board or circuit?

Do you work on existing products and prototypes?

Selected troubleshooting, documentation, CAD, and revision tasks can be considered. Send a short description of the application, operating conditions, and the help needed so suitability can be assessed before any work is accepted.

What should I send for a project request?

Start with the intended use, project goal, available information, budget, target timing, and the output you need. Include known power requirements and testing needs where relevant. Mention confidentiality requirements before sending sensitive design files.
