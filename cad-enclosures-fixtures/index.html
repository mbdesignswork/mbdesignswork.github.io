Design around the real assembly

A useful enclosure or fixture starts with the parts it needs to hold. Board dimensions, mounting holes, connector locations, cable access and the intended assembly process all affect the design.

Work can start from sketches, measured dimensions, a board model or existing CAD files. The goal may be a first-fit enclosure, a revised bracket, a holder for a PCB or a fixture for a defined bench task.

CAD and prototype deliverables

  • Enclosures: prototype housings, lids, mounting features and access for the agreed connectors and controls.
  • Small parts: brackets, clips, PCB holders and support parts for electronic assemblies.
  • Fixtures: test jigs, programming fixtures and assembly aids for defined workflows.
  • File preparation: STEP and STL exports, model cleanup and agreed fabrication or printing files.
  • Fit checks: prototype printing support, assembly checks and revision planning.

The chosen material, manufacturing process and required tolerances are part of the scope. Design labor, print material, print time and outside fabrication are separate unless included in a written quote.

Physical fit needs verification

CAD can help check placement and clearances, but a prototype is still useful for evaluating the actual fit, connector access and assembly sequence. Any strength, thermal, sealing or other performance requirements need their own agreed checks.

The homepage includes interactive CAD fixture and enclosure/bracket concept models. These illustrate the type of mechanical support work offered; the suitability of a new part is assessed for its own application.

Remote collaboration and next steps

Design files, dimensions and reviews can be exchanged with clients worldwide. Any physical samples, printing, shipping and time-zone requirements are agreed for the project.

Send the part's purpose, approximate dimensions, mounting points, available PCB or assembly models, connector details and intended manufacturing process. Explain what must fit and what needs to remain accessible. If the board itself needs changes, PCB layout support can be scoped alongside the CAD work.
